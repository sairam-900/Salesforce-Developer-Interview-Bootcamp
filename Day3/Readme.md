## 💡 What I Learned
Day 3 helped me understand how Salesforce automation tools solve different business requirements. I learned when to use Validation Rules for enforcing data quality, Record-Triggered Flows for automating business processes, and Apex Triggers for handling complex logic that cannot be achieved declaratively. I also gained hands-on experience building Flows, creating Validation Rules, and improving the Placement Management System with automation. Additionally, I strengthened my understanding of interview-focused concepts such as Flow vs Trigger, Workflow vs Process Builder vs Flow, and choosing the right automation tool for each scenario.

---

## ⚡ Challenges Faced
- Deciding whether to use a Validation Rule, Flow, or Apex Trigger for different requirements.
- Designing efficient Record-Triggered Flows.
- Writing Validation Rule formulas correctly.
- Understanding the execution order of Salesforce automation.
- Preventing duplicate applications without unnecessary Apex code.
- Choosing declarative automation before custom development.

---

## 🪞 Reflection
Day 3 emphasized Salesforce's "clicks before code" approach. I learned that many business requirements can be solved using declarative tools such as Validation Rules and Flows, reducing the need for Apex. Building automation for the Placement Management System improved my understanding of real-world Salesforce development and strengthened my confidence in answering automation-related interview questions. This session reinforced the importance of selecting the right Salesforce tool based on scalability, maintainability, and business needs.
